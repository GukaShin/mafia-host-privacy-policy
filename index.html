1. Introduction

Welcome to Mafia Host, a party-style social deduction game for Android users. Your privacy is important to us.

2. Data We Collect

Mafia Host does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the app. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location, and we do not run our own user database.

The app does include the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). That SDK collects and transmits device or other IDs off the device, as described in the next section.

3. Device or other IDs

This section matches the Google Play data type Device or other IDs.

Mafia Host uses Google AdMob to show advertisements, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads. When an advertisement is requested, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and send the following off your device to Google:

  • Device or other IDs, including the Android advertising ID (also called AAID or GAID) and related device identifiers used to request and measure ads
  • Device information needed to serve an ad
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown or clicked

Collected: yes. Collection is automatic when the app loads advertisements. We do not collect these identifiers ourselves in our own code or store them in an account.

Shared with third parties: yes. Device or other IDs and the related ad data above are shared with Google (AdMob), a third-party advertising provider. We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with any party other than Google’s advertising services.

How it is used: to display ads, measure whether ads were shown, limit repeated ads, and help prevent advertising fraud. It is not used to build a Mafia Host user profile.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalization, in your device settings (often Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads). Watching rewarded ads is optional. The game can still be played if an advertisement does not load.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Rewarded Ads

Watching rewarded ads is optional.

The game works offline except when loading advertisements.

5. Local Device Storage

Gameplay-related information such as player names, selected roles, and temporary session settings may be stored locally on the device only.

6. AI-Generated Audio

Some audio or voice content may be generated using AI tools or text-to-speech technologies.

7. Children's Privacy

Mafia Host is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising identifiers may still be processed by Google AdMob as described in section 3.

8. Analytics and Tracking

Mafia Host does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Ad measurement that uses device or other IDs is performed only by Google AdMob, as described in section 3.

9. Purchases

Mafia Host does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

10. Data Security

We do not operate servers that store player accounts or gameplay data. Device or other IDs and advertising data described in section 3 are transmitted by the AdMob SDK to Google. Player names, selected roles, and session settings stay on the device.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time.
